## Slide 2: The Healthcare Access Challenge in Pakistan
- **Title:** The Healthcare Challenge
- **Headline Message:** Millions of Pakistani families face immense hurdles accessing quality care.
- **Key Bullets:**
  1. 77% of healthcare spending is paid directly out-of-pocket (World Bank, 2024).
  2. 70% of doctors practice in major cities (Economic Survey 2023-24).
  3. Patients wait 2 to 3 hours in clinics (Lancet 2023).
  4. Finding authentic, verified specialists remains difficult and stressful for families.
  5. Travel drains household savings for routine follow-ups.
- **Visual:** Infographic highlighting the 77% out-of-pocket burden and urban-rural specialist gap.
- **Speaker Notes:**  
  Healthcare in Pakistan places a crushing burden on everyday families. More than three-quarters of medical costs come straight from household savings, yet most specialists are concentrated in a few cities. Patients travel long distances only to sit for hours in crowded waiting rooms.
